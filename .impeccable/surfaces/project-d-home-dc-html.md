# Surface: FRitZ catalog (public) + admin

Scope: every artboard on the canvas. Catalog home, list, product, compare, wizard are Persuade/Operate hybrids (home = Persuade, the rest Operate); admin = Operate.
Audience: homeowners on phones; installers searching by model code; internal team in admin.
Action: build an inquiry list and send it on WhatsApp. Constraints: no price, no stock, no cart; IP/Wi-Fi absent; Kalameh only; lime #AFCB08 for emphasis.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The product is the page. A European-maker catalog — white ground, one large product image per view, Persian type set large and quiet — refusing the bordered card grid, badges and dashboard chrome of local electronics shops.

OWN-WORLD: Ground #FFFFFF with one warm grey tone step (#F4F4F2) instead of lines; ink #141414; lime #AFCB08 only on the single primary action, the inquiry count and the active state. Kalameh in two sizes doing the work (display 56–88, text 17). Buttons full pill; surfaces square-cornered tone fields; no shadows at rest, no 1px outline boxes.

STORY: The visitor sees that FRitZ carries many brands and protocols, finds a product by need or by code, trusts the datasheet and adds it to the list.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Headline (2 lines max, 68px desktop) and one sentence at the start side; one dominant product image filling the other 7/12 on a grey field at 680px tall; the one lime button (product finder) under the copy; search sits in the sticky nav.

FORM: Category canon (standing exit), played straight. seed:06ae83c8afc94a04
